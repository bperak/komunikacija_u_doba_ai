<div class="impresum-section impresum-author-note">
<div class="impresum-label">About the author</div>
<p><strong>Assoc. Prof. Benedikt Perak, PhD</strong> is Associate Professor at the Department of Cultural Studies, Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences, University of Rijeka, where he heads the Laboratory for Research on Cultural Complexity. He is co-founder of <strong><a href="https://syntagent.com">SyntAgent</a></strong> d.o.o. (Rijeka), a company specialising in artificial intelligence, language technologies, and data science. His research spans natural language processing, cognitive and computational linguistics, and computational approaches to cultural and communicative complexity; he has authored and co-authored numerous publications in language technologies and semantic analysis.</p>
