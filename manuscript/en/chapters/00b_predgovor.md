## Foreword

This book continues reflection shaped through the courses *Language, Thought, and Culture*, *Data Science in Cultural Studies*, and *Artificial Intelligence and Communication in Culture* at the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences in Rijeka. It aims to contribute to understanding changes in communicative practices in the age of artificial intelligence, drawing on relevant literature and a critical stance. It is intended for students of communication, linguistics, cultural studies and related fields, as well as for readers who wish to follow systematically how we communicate with systems whose linguistic capabilities grow ever more sophisticated.

Although the book as a medium no longer occupies the central place it once did, this project adopts a form that is systematically renewed — an **agentically renewable book**. The first edition will gradually be extended with agent-assisted information retrieval, integration of new *state-of-the-art* technological solutions, and critical discussion of their implications for communicative and cultural phenomena. That process remains under the author’s responsibility and control.

In that spirit, new versions of this renewable book will be published periodically on [GitHub](https://github.com/bperak/komunikacija_u_doba_ai), as a concrete embodiment of its core idea: we are on the threshold of collaborative work with artificial intelligence agents.

I thank colleagues who encouraged me and made disciplined research experimentation possible, from which the insights in this book emerged. I owe special gratitude to my family and friends for their steady support and for the sense of meaningfulness they provide.
